The final large scientific answer identifies ten kinds of missing work in explanations of consciousness. First comes definition: having experience, attending, remembering, reporting, reflecting, and sustaining a self are not automatically the same phenomenon. Second comes the unresolved spatial and temporal grain of the physical substrate: individual cells, particular cell types, layers, recurrent local circuits, thalamocortical loops, broad networks, biochemical processes, fields, or combinations of these.

Fifth comes unity and binding. Distributed processing must somehow be related to one experienced scene and one subject, while other simultaneous activity remains unconscious.
